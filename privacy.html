Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains how Companero Resort handles information when you use this website to contact us or request accommodation or dining. A request is not a confirmed reservation or booking.

Information you provide

Depending on the form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, message or special requests, requested check-in and check-out dates, room and guest counts, or requested dining date, time, and party size. Please do not include payment-card details, passwords, or sensitive personal information in a message.

How we use your information

We use inquiry details to respond to you, review availability, follow up about your request, and manage guest communications. Submitting a request does not reserve a room or table, guarantee availability, or charge you.

Storage and who can access it

When you submit a form, the details are stored in the resort's website database and are available to authorized staff through the staff inbox. The website may send a copy of a request to the resort's staff email address when email notifications are enabled. Email and hosting providers may process information to deliver those services. Email can create additional copies outside the website database.

Retention and security

We keep inquiry records only for as long as they are needed to handle the request, maintain appropriate business records, and meet applicable legal requirements. Records should be deleted when they are no longer needed for those purposes. The website also keeps limited, keyed hashes of network addresses temporarily to control automated form submissions and staff sign-in attempts; expired rate-limit records are periodically removed.

Access to inquiry records is restricted to authorized staff. The staff sign-in uses a session cookie configured as Secure, HttpOnly, and SameSite=Strict and expires after one hour of inactivity. The Secure setting requires HTTPS. Do not send sensitive information through the contact form.

Cookies, analytics, and external services

The public pages do not currently use analytics or marketing cookies. A session cookie is used for staff sign-in; it is not used to track visitors across websites. Some page images are delivered by Unsplash and the site uses fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to those providers to load those assets. If you follow a Google Maps or WhatsApp link, those services handle your visit under their own privacy policies.

Your questions and requests

To ask about the personal information you have sent us, or to request its correction or deletion, email Companeroresort.sl@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request before taking action. The rights available to you depend on the laws that apply where you live.

For questions about the resort or an inquiry, contact us at +232 30 555 551 or WhatsApp +232 78 767 876.
